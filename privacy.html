BDC Warmer

BDC Warmer data notice

Effective October 19, 2026

BDC Warmer is our private Google app, used only with our own accounts. It accesses each connected account's name, email address and Gmail, and uses them only to send, read, reply to and organize messages between our accounts and to check each account's age and mail delivery. It keeps account details, encrypted sign-ins and records of those messages on our own computer. It never sells Google data or uses it for ads, and shares it only to run the app or when the law requires it.

BDC Warmer's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

To remove its access, visit myaccount.google.com/connections. To have anything it stored deleted, email blaize@berrydesignco.com.
