How it works

Three steps, and you only talk to me.

No account to set up and no big order to commit to.

  1. 1

    I design your card

    I look at your business and put together a postcard with your name, your colors, your offer and your phone number.

  2. 2

    We make it yours

    Want a different offer, photo or line? Just tell me and I'll change it until it's right. Nothing prints until you say so.

  3. 3

    We print it and mail it

    Our family print shop prints your cards and mails them to every home on the routes you pick, or to your own customer list.
